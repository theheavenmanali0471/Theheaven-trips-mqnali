DAY 01

Manali Local

Hadimba Temple → Vashisht Hot Springs → Van Vihar → Mall Road. Local culture, temple architecture and evening market.

DAY 02

Solang Valley → Snow Point → Atal Tunnel → Sissu

Adventure activities where available, followed by the Atal Tunnel/Lahaul side route. Snow and road access are seasonal and condition-dependent.

DAY 03

Kullu Valley Experience

Paragliding point → River Rafting point → Shawl Factory → local shopping → Vaishno Devi Temple. Optional Kasol/Manikaran can be discussed according to time.

DAY 04

Manali → Lahaul / Keylong or Jispa

Scenic drive through Atal Tunnel → Sissu → Tandi/Keylong/Jispa side depending on the selected plan and road conditions. Overnight stay is recommended for a comfortable high-altitude journey.

DAY 05

High-Mountain Extension — Baralacha La

Keylong/Jispa side → high-altitude route towards Baralacha La when officially accessible → scenic stops → return as per road status. Chandratal/Shinkhula are better planned as separate extended routes rather than forcing them into one short day.

* Activities and high-altitude routes are seasonal/condition-dependent. Do not treat the activity list as a guarantee of availability. Check current local conditions before departure.
